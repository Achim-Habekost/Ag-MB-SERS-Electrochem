# Experimental data

This directory contains experimental data associated with the Ag–methylene blue SERS study.

The datasets will include:
- Cyclic voltammograms recorded at Au and Ag electrodes.
- A control voltammogram of Ag in 0.1 M KCl without methylene blue.
- Potential-resolved Raman/SERS measurements of methylene blue.

Electrode potentials are reported versus the Ag pseudo-reference unless otherwise specified.

Individual datasets and their measurement conditions will be documented alongside the corresponding files.
